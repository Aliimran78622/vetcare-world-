Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Please read this disclaimer carefully before using any information on VetCare World. It explains the limits of the veterinary information published on this website.

1. Educational Information Only

All content on VetCare World — including articles, guides, videos, PDFs, posters and community discussions — is published for general education and information only. It is designed to help farmers, livestock owners and veterinary students learn about animal health. It is not veterinary medical advice, diagnosis or treatment for any specific animal.

2. Not a Substitute for Professional Veterinary Advice

The information on this website does not replace examination, diagnosis or treatment by a qualified veterinarian who can see and examine your animal. Every animal, farm and situation is different. What is correct for one animal may not be correct for another. Never delay professional care because of something you read on this website.

3. Always Consult a Qualified Veterinarian

Before starting, changing or stopping any treatment, medicine, vaccine or feeding practice, always consult a qualified veterinarian in your own area. This is especially important for sick animals, pregnant animals, young calves, and animals used for milk or meat production, where drug withdrawal periods and local regulations apply.

4. No Diagnosis or Treatment Guarantee

VetCare World does not diagnose animals online and does not guarantee that any treatment described on this website will work in a particular case. Treatment outcomes depend on correct diagnosis, the animal's condition, the stage of disease, proper administration and follow-up care by a veterinarian.

5. Emergency Situations

If an animal is seriously ill, injured, in severe pain, having difficulty breathing, unable to stand, bleeding heavily, or in a difficult delivery, contact a local veterinarian immediately. Do not wait for an online reply. Emergency care must be given in person, as quickly as possible.

6. Drug Doses and Treatment Information

Where doses or treatment protocols are mentioned, they are standard reference values for educational purposes only. Doses vary with the animal's species, weight, age, condition and local drug availability and regulations. Always follow the prescription and instructions of your own veterinarian, and read the medicine label. Never use medicines without professional guidance, and never use expired or unlabelled products.

7. Ask the Vet and Community Answers

Questions submitted through Ask the Vet and answers shared in the community are general educational guidance only. They are not a substitute for a veterinary examination. A reply given without seeing the animal cannot be relied upon as a diagnosis or a treatment plan.

8. Accuracy of Content

We work to publish accurate, science-based information, and our content is prepared by a registered veterinary practitioner. However, veterinary knowledge changes over time, and errors are possible. We do not warrant that the content is complete, current or free of mistakes.

9. No Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, VetCare World and its owner are not liable for any loss, damage, cost or harm — including harm to animals — arising from reliance on the content of this website or from any action taken, or not taken, based on it. Decisions about animal health remain the responsibility of the animal's owner and their veterinarian.

10. Contact
