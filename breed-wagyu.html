Introduction

Wagyu means simply 'Japanese cow'. Behind the name hides the most marbled, tender and expensive beef the world has ever known.

Fine white fat spreads through the meat like snowflakes. Top-grade Wagyu sells for over $100 per kilo.

Wagyu at a Glance

TypeBeef breed (luxury)

Varieties & Types of Wagyu

Wagyu is not one breed but four Japanese breeds:

  • Japanese Black — The main Wagyu breed — over 90% of Wagyu. Supreme marbling and size. The Tajima strain of Hyogo gives the world-famous Kobe beef.
  • Japanese Brown — A rarer, browner breed — leaner meat than the Black.
  • Japanese Shorthorn — A rare northern breed, kept in small numbers.
  • Japanese Polled — The rarest Wagyu breed — naturally hornless.

Characteristics

  • Deep Body — A deep, well-sprung body carrying the marbling genetics.
  • Black Coat — Solid black in the Black breed; brown in the Brown.
  • Strong Frame — Medium-large frame, grown slowly for quality.
  • Horned Head — Medium horns, curving forward — usually kept horned.

Strengths

  • Most valuable beef in the world
  • Extreme marbling genetics
  • Calm, quiet temperament
  • High price per animal
  • F1 crosses upgrade any herd

Keep in Mind

Wagyu grow slowly and the genetics are expensive — this is a patient, premium-market breed, not a fast-turnover one. Fullblood cows can cost up to about US $30,000.

Beef Production

Wagyu dress out at an ordinary 60–65% — the yield is normal; the value is in the marbling. Top A5-grade beef sells above US $100 per kilo.

F1 Wagyu crosses (Wagyu × other breeds) transform the beef quality of ordinary herds at a much lower cost than fullblood stock.

Price & Buying Tips

Typical price — international (indicative): US $3,000 – 30,000 — fullblood breeding stock; top cows reach the top of the range. All prices are indicative and move with the market.

Prices vary hugely by bloodline and marbling genetics. Australia and the USA hold the biggest herds outside Japan; embryos and semen are costly.

Before You Buy — Checklist

  • Body frame & muscling
  • Legs & hooves sound
  • Breeding organs normal
  • Teeth & age checked
  • Body condition score 3+
  • Vaccination & deworming record
  • Temperament calm
  • Weight on scale, not by eye

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the four Wagyu breeds?

Japanese Black (over 90% of Wagyu), Japanese Brown, Japanese Shorthorn and Japanese Polled. The Black — especially the Tajima strain behind Kobe beef — is king.

Why is Wagyu beef so expensive?

Extreme marbling: fine fat woven through the meat gives unmatched tenderness and flavour. Top A5-grade beef sells for over US $100 per kg.

How much does a Wagyu cow cost?

Fullblood breeding stock ranges roughly US $3,000–30,000 (indicative); top cows reach the top of the range. F1 crosses are much cheaper.

Are Wagyu kept outside Japan?

Yes — Australia and the USA hold the biggest herds outside Japan, and Wagyu genetics trade worldwide.
