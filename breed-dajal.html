Introduction

The Dajal is the draught breed of southern Punjab — Dera Ghazi Khan and Rajanpur — a grey-white zebu bred purely for power.

Dajal bullocks are prized for ploughing and cart work in the hot Indus plains. Cows give modest milk; this breed's fame is strength.

Dajal at a Glance

OriginDera Ghazi Khan & Rajanpur, South Punjab, Pakistan
TypeDraught breed (heavy work)
Milk per lactation700–1,100 litres (reported)
CoatGrey to white
FameHeavy ploughing in hard soils; proudly decorated bullocks
Price in Pakistan (indicative)PKR 150,000–350,000 per animal

Characteristics

  • The Hump — A strong hump over powerful working shoulders.
  • Grey-White Coat — Grey to white coat suited to the hot plains.
  • Strong Hindquarters — Heavy hindquarters built for pulling power.
  • Decorated Collar — Farmers proudly decorate their best bullocks.

Strengths

  • Very strong draught power
  • Hardy in extreme heat
  • Works on poor feed
  • Long working life
  • Proud festive tradition

Keep in Mind

Milk is a side benefit (700–1,100 litres reported) and records for the breed vary — the Dajal is selected and priced for work, not milk.

Milk & Meat Production

Heavy ploughing in hard soils and strong, steady cart work are the breed’s products. Milk is modest — 700–1,100 litres reported per lactation — a side benefit while draught is the main job.

Trained, matched bullock pairs are the pride of South Punjab farms and sell at a premium.

Price & Buying Tips

Indicative price in Pakistan: PKR 150,000 – 350,000 — per animal; trained bullock pairs cost more. All prices are indicative and move with the market.

Trained, matched pairs sell at a premium. Prices move with the local mandi and the animal’s strength and training.

Before You Buy — Checklist

  • Frame, legs & hooves sound
  • Pulling power tested at work
  • Teeth & age checked
  • Body condition score 3+
  • Vaccination & deworming record
  • Temperament calm & trained
  • Weight on scale, not by eye

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Dajal breed used for?

Heavy draught work — ploughing hard soils and carting in the hot Indus plains of South Punjab.

Where does the Dajal breed come from?

Dera Ghazi Khan and Rajanpur districts of southern Punjab, Pakistan.

How much milk does a Dajal cow give?

Modest milk — about 700–1,100 litres per lactation (reported). Work comes first in this breed.

What is the price of a Dajal animal in Pakistan?

Indicatively PKR 150,000–350,000 per animal; trained, matched bullock pairs cost more.
