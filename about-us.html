About Dr. Shahid Bilal

Dr. Shahid Bilal Depal is a Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM, FVS) from the University of Veterinary and Animal Sciences (UVAS), Lahore, where he studied from 2015 to 2020. He is a Registered Veterinary Medical Practitioner with the Pakistan Veterinary Medical Council (PVMC), registration number RVMP 15068, and has been practicing as a veterinarian since 2020.

He runs his veterinary clinic, Depal Veterinary Clinic (ڈیپال شفاء خانہ حیوانات) — Total Farm Solution — at Jalalpur Pirwala Interchange, Multan, Punjab, Pakistan, along with Markazi Nasal Kashi Haiwanat (Central Animal Breeding), where he treats animals and helps farmers with breeding and herd health. His expertise covers livestock management, breeding, fattening, and treatment.

Alongside his veterinary work, Dr. Bilal is also a livestock trader and a farmer. He does fattening and dairy farming himself, and his daily field work keeps him close to the real problems farmers face — from feeding and breeding to disease treatment.

He founded VetCare World to share this practical, field-tested knowledge with farmers, vet students, and animal owners around the world — through the website, YouTube, and Facebook. His belief is simple: healthy animals mean prosperous farmers.
