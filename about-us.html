About VetCare World

VetCare World is an educational livestock knowledge and community platform created to support farmers, breeders, animal keepers and veterinary professionals. Our aim is to make practical animal-health and farm-management learning easier to access around the world.

Our learning topics include livestock diseases and prevention, vaccination, feed and nutrition, breeds and breeding, calf care and farm management. We aim to make useful information accessible in English and Urdu.

Our Mission

To connect livestock communities and encourage responsible animal care through learning, knowledge sharing and collaboration.

Our Content Principles

  • Share educational information in clear language.
  • Encourage animal welfare, prevention and responsible farming.
  • Respect users and moderate inappropriate community content.
  • Keep improving the platform as it develops.

Important veterinary notice: Website content is for general educational purposes. It is not a substitute for an examination, diagnosis or treatment plan from a qualified veterinarian. For a sick or emergency animal, contact a local veterinary professional.
