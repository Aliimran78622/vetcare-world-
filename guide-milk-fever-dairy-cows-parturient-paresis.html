Free illustrated guide to Milk Fever (Parturient Paresis / hypocalcemia) in dairy cows — low blood calcium around calving that leaves the cow weak, down and unable to stand. This 17-page VetCare World guide explains the causes and risk factors, the 3 stages of milk fever, clinical signs with photos, diagnosis (blood calcium test), emergency treatment with IV calcium borogluconate, subclinical hypocalcemia, prevention (DCAD, diet and management) and complications — with pictures on every page.
