Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an online veterinary consultation?

Fill the booking form on this page, pay the fee (Rs 500 in Pakistan via JazzCash or bank transfer), enter your transaction ID, then tap the WhatsApp button — it opens with your booking details ready to send. Photos or a short video of the animal help the doctor advise you better.

How much is the consultation fee?

The fee is Rs 500 per consultation in Pakistan and $2 per consultation for international clients.

How do I pay the fee?

In Pakistan, pay Rs 500 via JazzCash or bank transfer. International clients pay $2 via Payoneer. The payment details are shown in step 2 of the booking form. Your payment is verified in our account before the consultation starts — please send your payment screenshot on WhatsApp.

What happens during the consultation?

Dr. Shahid Bilal Depal reviews your animal's symptoms, photos or video and advises on diagnosis, treatment, medicines, feeding and management over WhatsApp chat or call.

Is online consultation a replacement for a vet visit?

No. Online consultation is professional guidance based on the information, photos and videos you share. It does not replace an in-person examination, and serious cases should be seen by a local veterinarian.

What if my animal is in an emergency?

Call your nearest veterinarian immediately. Online consultation is not for life-threatening emergencies — do not wait for an online reply when an animal needs urgent care.
