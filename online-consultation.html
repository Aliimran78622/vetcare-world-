How It Works

  1. Message on WhatsApp — describe your animal, its age, symptoms and what you have tried so far. Photos or a short video help a lot.
  2. Pay the fee — payment details are shared on WhatsApp when you book.
  3. Consultation — Dr. Shahid reviews your case and advises you over WhatsApp chat or call.
  4. Advice & follow-up — you receive clear guidance on diagnosis, treatment, medicines, feeding or management, and what to watch for next.

What You Can Ask About

  • Sick animals — diagnosis guidance, treatment plans and medicine advice
  • Calf care — colostrum, feeding, diarrhoea and pneumonia
  • Reproduction — heat detection, AI timing, pregnancy and infertility
  • Feeding & nutrition — rations, minerals, silage and TMR
  • Udder health — mastitis prevention and treatment
  • Emergency first aid — what to do while you reach your local vet
  • Farm management — breeding, housing and disease prevention
⚠️ Important — please read

Online consultation is professional guidance based on the information, photos and videos you share. It does not replace an in-person examination by a veterinarian. Serious cases should always be seen by your local veterinarian. In an emergency, call your nearest veterinarian immediately — do not wait for an online reply. No outcome can be guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an online veterinary consultation?

Send a WhatsApp message to 0322-7211322 describing your animal, its age, symptoms and what you have tried so far. Photos or a short video of the animal help the doctor advise you better.

How much is the consultation fee?

The fee is Rs 500 per consultation in Pakistan and $2 per consultation for international clients.

How do I pay the fee?

Payment details are shared on WhatsApp when you book your consultation.

What happens during the consultation?

Dr. Shahid Bilal Depal reviews your animal's symptoms, photos or video and advises on diagnosis, treatment, medicines, feeding and management over WhatsApp chat or call.

Is online consultation a replacement for a vet visit?

No. Online consultation is professional guidance based on the information, photos and videos you share. It does not replace an in-person examination, and serious cases should be seen by a local veterinarian.

What if my animal is in an emergency?

Call your nearest veterinarian immediately. Online consultation is not for life-threatening emergencies — do not wait for an online reply when an animal needs urgent care.
