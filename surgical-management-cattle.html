VetCare World Surgical Management Learning Series

About This Series

Surgery is a core part of cattle veterinary practice — from routine procedures like dehorning and castration to life-saving operations like caesarean section and rumenotomy. This series covers 20 surgical procedures in cattle with complete, practical guides.

20 complete guides in this series. Each guide includes indications, surgical techniques with step-by-step pictures, instruments, complications, postoperative care and prevention.

For: veterinary students, veterinary doctors, livestock farmers and animal breeders worldwide.

⚠️ Important: all surgical procedures must be performed by a qualified veterinarian under proper anaesthesia and aseptic conditions. The information here is educational and does not replace professional veterinary examination and treatment.
