Introduction

The Gir comes from the Gir forest region of Gujarat, India — a noble zebu with a round forehead and long, hanging ears.

Brazil built a dairy empire on Gir blood. Its milk is rich, its ghee famous, and its A2 milk is in growing demand worldwide.

Varieties & Types of Gir

The breed is seen in two colour forms:

  • Red Gir — Deep red is the classic Gir colour — rich, glossy and striking.
  • Speckled Gir — Many Gir carry white speckles and patches over the red base coat.

Characteristics

  • The Dewlap — Long, loose dewlap helps the body shed heat.
  • Red Coat — Deep red, often speckled with white patches.
  • The Hump — A clear zebu hump over the shoulders.
  • Long Ears & Horns — Long hanging ears; horns curve back like a half-moon.

Strengths

  • Rich milk, famous A2 ghee
  • Heat and disease tolerant
  • Long, productive life
  • Calm, noble temperament
  • Valuable breeding genetics

Keep in Mind

Under village feeding the yield sits at the lower end of the range — selected Brazilian Gir herds pass 4,000 litres, which shows what good feeding and selection can do.

Milk & Meat Production

The Gir gives 1,200–3,000 litres of milk per lactation, rich milk with fat commonly 4.5% or more. Selected Brazilian Gir herds pass 4,000 litres.

Gir is the base of Brazil's Girolando dairy cross, and its A2 milk and famous ghee are in growing demand.

Price & Buying Tips

Typical price — international (indicative): US $800 – 2,500 — per breeding animal; elite dairy families cost more. All prices are indicative and move with the market.

Prices vary by country, milk record and pedigree. Brazil pays top prices for Gir genetics, and top Brazilian lines sell well above this range.

Before You Buy — Checklist

  • Milk record of the cow & mother
  • Udder — 4 working quarters
  • Teats — no lumps or sores
  • Hooves & legs sound
  • Body condition score 2.75–3.25
  • Vaccination & deworming record
  • Pregnancy status confirmed
  • Milk let-down tested

Frequently Asked Questions

How much milk does a Gir cow give?

Typically 1,200–3,000 litres per lactation, with rich milk (fat commonly 4.5%+). Selected herds in Brazil exceed 4,000 litres.

Why is Gir milk famous?

It is rich A2-type milk, famous for its ghee — and demand for A2 milk is growing worldwide.

How can you recognise a Gir?

By the round, bulging (convex) forehead, long hanging ears and horns that curve back like a half-moon.

What is the price of a Gir animal?

About US $800–2,500 per breeding animal (indicative); elite dairy families cost more.
