About This Series

Theriogenology is the science of animal reproduction — breeding, pregnancy, birth and the genetics that shape the next generation. This series covers the 10 major topics of modern theriogenology, from artificial insemination to genomics and sustainable breeding.

10 complete guides in this series. Each guide includes complete theoretical notes, clinical procedures with step-by-step pictures, practical field guidelines, treatment and management protocols, diagrams and the latest scientific developments.

For: veterinary students, veterinary doctors, livestock farmers, animal breeders and researchers worldwide.

Our mission: to promote modern animal reproduction, improve livestock genetics, develop productive and climate-resilient breeds, and contribute to sustainable livestock farming worldwide.
