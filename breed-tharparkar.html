Introduction

The Tharparkar takes its name from the Thar desert and the Parkar region of Sindh — a white-grey zebu of great endurance.

She gives useful milk and her bullocks are strong draught workers. In drought years, when other breeds fail, the Tharparkar carries on.

Tharparkar at a Glance

OriginTharparkar, Sindh (Thar desert) — also Jodhpur, India
TypeDual-purpose: milk + draught
Milk per lactation1,200–2,000 litres
CoatWhite to light grey; bulls darken with age
HornsTall, curving up like a lyre
Price in Pakistan (indicative)PKR 180,000–450,000 per milking cow

Characteristics

  • The Udder — Medium, neat udder — a working dairy zebu.
  • White-Grey Coat — White to light grey; bulls darken with age.
  • Medium Frame — Strong, medium frame built for walking far.
  • Lyre Horns — Tall horns curving up like a lyre.

Strengths

  • Drought and heat survivor
  • Milk plus draught bullocks
  • Walks far for grazing
  • Hardy, low-input keeper
  • Calm desert temperament

Keep in Mind

Milk yield is moderate — the Tharparkar is valued for carrying on through drought, not for breaking records. Good farm herds reach the top of the range.

Milk & Meat Production

The Tharparkar gives 1,200–2,000 litres of milk per lactation, and her milk holds up even in drought conditions.

Male calves grow into strong draught bullocks — the second income of this true dual-purpose desert breed.

Price & Buying Tips

Indicative price in Pakistan: PKR 180,000 – 450,000 — per milking cow, depending on milk yield & pregnancy. All prices are indicative and move with the market.

Prices move with the local mandi and milk rates. The breed is common in Sindh mandis, bullocks are priced for work, and good milkers near Karachi sell at the top of the range.

Before You Buy — Checklist

  • Milk record of the mother
  • Frame, legs & hooves sound
  • Udder — 4 working quarters
  • Body condition score 3+
  • Vaccination & deworming record
  • Pregnancy status confirmed
  • Temperament calm
  • Weight on scale, not by eye

Frequently Asked Questions

How much milk does a Tharparkar give?

Typically 1,200–2,000 litres per lactation — and the milk holds up even in drought years.

What is the price of a Tharparkar cow in Pakistan?

Indicatively PKR 180,000–450,000 per milking cow, depending on yield and pregnancy.

Why is the Tharparkar called a desert breed?

It comes from the Thar desert of Sindh and is built for endurance — walking far, eating little and surviving drought.

Is the Tharparkar dual-purpose?

Yes — cows give useful milk and bullocks are strong draught workers.
