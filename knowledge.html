Welcome to Livestock Knowledge Centre

VetCare World provides educational information for livestock farmers, veterinary professionals, breeders and animal lovers around the world.

Explore animal health, disease prevention, cattle breeding, nutrition, vaccination and modern farm management.

1. Livestock Disease and Treatment

Learn about common cattle and buffalo diseases, clinical signs, diagnosis, prevention and veterinary treatment principles.

2. Vaccination and Disease Prevention

Understand vaccination programmes, herd health planning, biosecurity and preventive veterinary care.

3. Animal Feed and Nutrition

Explore balanced animal diets, fodder, silage, minerals, clean drinking water and nutritional management.

4. Breeds and Animal Breeding

Discover cattle and buffalo breeds, artificial insemination, reproductive health and genetic improvement.

5. Farm Management

Learn about dairy farm management, calf care, animal housing, hygiene, productivity and livestock welfare.

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect farmers, veterinary doctors, breeders and livestock communities through reliable knowledge and global collaboration.
